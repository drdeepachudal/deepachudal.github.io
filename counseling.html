The 5 Pillars of Preconception Optimization

1. Medical Assessment

Chronic disease management (diabetes, thyroid, hypertension), medication review, immunization status (rubella, varicella), and oral health evaluation.

2. Genetic Screening

Carrier screening for thalassemia, cystic fibrosis, spinal muscular atrophy. Family history evaluation for inherited conditions.

3. Nutritional Optimization

Folic acid (400-800mcg), iron, vitamin D, iodine, and omega-3 supplementation. BMI optimization (18.5-24.9).

4. Lifestyle Modification

Smoking cessation, alcohol elimination, caffeine reduction (<200mg/day), stress management, and sleep hygiene.

5. Fertility & Cycle Optimization

Ovulation tracking, semen analysis (if indicated), hormonal assessment, and timing of conception window.

What's Included in a Preconception Consultation?

  • Detailed medical & obstetric history review
  • Complete physical examination & BMI assessment
  • Blood tests: CBC, blood group, Rubella IgG, Hepatitis B, HIV, VDRL

Advanced Preconception Genetic Screening (2025-2026)

Expanded Carrier Screening (ECS)

Screens for 500+ autosomal recessive & X-linked disorders. ACOG recommends offering to all couples planning pregnancy, regardless of ethnicity.

PGT-P (Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Polygenic Risk)

Emerging technology to assess polygenic risk scores for common adult-onset conditions. Ethical preconception discussion available.

Non-Invasive Prenatal Screening (NIPS) Preconception

Some centers now offer NIPS before pregnancy to assess egg donor or sperm donor genetic profiles.

Fragile X & Spinal Muscular Atrophy

Targeted screening for high-risk populations. Dr. Chudal provides pretest and post-test genetic counseling.

Evidence-Based Nutrition & Lifestyle Optimization

What you eat and how you live directly impacts egg quality, sperm health, and implantation. Dr. Chudal provides personalized recommendations based on current evidence:

  • Mediterranean-style diet: High in folate, antioxidants, omega-3s. Associated with higher IVF success rates.
  • Omega-3 fatty acids: 200-300mg DHA daily improves endometrial receptivity.
  • Folate (not just folic acid): Methylfolate for MTHFR variants — 800mcg daily starting 3 months prior.
  • Exercise: Moderate activity 150 min/week. Avoid over-exercise which can disrupt ovulation.
  • Smoking cessation: Reduces miscarriage risk by 40% and improves egg quality within 3 months of quitting.

Preconception for Special Situations

Chronic Medical Conditions

Diabetes: optimize HbA1c <6.5%. Hypertension: switch to pregnancy-safe medications (labetalol, nifedipine). Thyroid: achieve TSH <2.5 mIU/L.

Previous Pregnancy Complications

History of preeclampsia, preterm birth, gestational diabetes, or recurrent loss — preconception risk reduction protocols.

Advanced Maternal Age (>35)

Ovarian reserve testing (AMH, AFC), CoQ10 supplementation, earlier fertility referral if trying for 6 months.

Medication Optimization

Review teratogenic medications (ACE inhibitors, statins, valproate, isotretinoin) and transition to safer alternatives 3 months prior.
