Nutrition for Hormonal Balance & Fertility

Anti-Inflammatory Fertility Diet

Mediterranean-style eating: leafy greens, berries, fatty fish (omega-3s), nuts, seeds, olive oil. Reduces oxidative stress and improves egg/sperm quality.

Key Fertility Nutrients

Folate (400-800mcg), Vitamin D (2000 IU), CoQ10 (200-400mg), Omega-3 (DHA 200-300mg), Zinc, Selenium. Customized supplementation plans.

Blood Sugar Balance

Low glycemic load meals, protein with every meal, fiber-rich foods. Critical for PCOS, insulin resistance, and metabolic health.

Gut Health & Hormones

Probiotics (Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium), prebiotic fiber, fermented foods. Gut microbiome affects estrogen metabolism (estrobolome).

Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Improves endometrial receptivity, reduces inflammation. Sources: salmon, sardines, walnuts, flaxseed. Supplement if dietary intake low.

Meal Timing & Circadian Rhythms

Time-restricted eating (12-14 hour overnight fast) improves metabolic health and mitochondrial function.

Lifestyle Medicine: Movement, Sleep & Stress Mastery

Physical Activity:

  • 150 minutes moderate exercise/week (brisk walking, swimming, cycling)
  • Strength training 2x/week — improves insulin sensitivity
  • Yoga and Pilates — reduces cortisol, improves pelvic blood flow
  • Avoid over-exercise (can disrupt ovulation)

Sleep Optimization:

  • 7-9 hours/night — regulates melatonin, cortisol, and reproductive hormones
  • Consistent sleep/wake schedule
  • Dark, cool bedroom; no screens 1 hour before bed

Stress Mastery Techniques

  • Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) — 8-week program
  • Daily meditation (10-20 minutes)
  • Deep breathing (4-7-8 technique, diaphragmatic breathing)
  • Progressive muscle relaxation
  • Nature exposure (forest bathing)
  • Journaling & gratitude practice
Research shows: Mindfulness programs improve IVF live birth rates by 30-40% (Hum Reprod 2025).

Mind-Body Connection: Fertility & Emotional Wellness

Stress & Fertility

Chronic stress elevates cortisol → disrupts GnRH pulsatility → ovulatory dysfunction, reduced libido, impaired implantation. Stress reduction is medical treatment.

Mindfulness-Based Fertility Programs

Dr. Chudal offers guided fertility meditation, relaxation techniques, and referrals to trained mental health professionals specializing in reproductive health.

Acupuncture & Fertility

Evidence supports acupuncture for improving uterine blood flow, reducing stress, and increasing IVF success rates (especially around embryo transfer).

Couples Communication & Support

Fertility journey impacts relationships. Strategies for shared decision-making, emotional support, and managing uncertainty together.

Fertility Optimization: For Women & Men

For Women

  • BMI optimization (18.5-24.9) — improves ovulation
  • Prenatal vitamins 3 months before TTC
  • Limit caffeine to <200mg/day
  • Avoid alcohol completely when TTC
  • Track cycles (BBT, LH strips, cervical mucus)
  • Address thyroid & prolactin abnormalities

For Men

  • Antioxidant-rich diet (zinc, selenium, CoQ10, vitamin C/E)
  • Avoid testicular overheating (no laptops on lap, loose underwear)
  • Quit smoking — improves sperm DNA integrity in 3 months
  • Limit alcohol (<2 drinks/day)
  • Exercise but avoid excessive cycling or heat exposure

Preconception Timeline

  • 3-6 months before TTC: Start folic acid, optimize weight, quit smoking, reduce alcohol, address chronic conditions
  • 2-3 months before: Complete genetic carrier screening, update immunizations
  • 1 month before: Track ovulation, reduce caffeine, manage stress
  • During TTC: Have intercourse every 1-2 days around fertile window (5 days before to 1 day after ovulation)
Did you know? Lifestyle changes take 3 months to impact egg quality (folliculogenesis) and 74 days for sperm (spermatogenesis).

Environmental Health: Reducing Toxin Exposure

Plastics & Endocrine Disruptors

Avoid BPA/BPS — use glass or stainless steel containers. Never microwave plastic. Reduce phthalates (avoid fragranced products).

Organic Produce & Pesticides

Prioritize organic for "Dirty Dozen" (strawberries, spinach, kale, apples). Wash all produce thoroughly.

Clean Personal Care Products

Avoid parabens, phthalates, triclosan in cosmetics and soaps. Choose EWG-verified products.

Home Air & Water Quality

Use HEPA filters, avoid synthetic fragrances, test water for heavy metals. Consider reverse osmosis for drinking water.
