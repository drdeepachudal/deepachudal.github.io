Major Scientific Breakthrough in IVF (2025-2026)

FEMI: Foundational IVF Model for Imaging

Published in Nature Communications (July 2025), researchers at Weill Cornell Medicine developed FEMI, a vision transformer AI trained on 18 million time-lapse embryo images. This foundational model predicts blastocyst quality, ploidy status, and developmental stages with unprecedented accuracy, reducing subjective variability in embryo selection. Our IVF laboratory at Nepal Police Hospital is adopting AI-assisted embryo monitoring to enhance live birth rates while reducing time-to-pregnancy. [reference:0]


Why it matters: AI integration allows embryologists to standardize embryo grading, increase pregnancy rates, and lower miscarriage risk related to aneuploidy.

Reference: Rajendran et al. “A foundational model for in vitro fertilization trained on 18 million time-lapse images.” Nature Communications 16, 2025.
