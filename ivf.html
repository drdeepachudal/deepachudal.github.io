Meet Dr. Deepa Chudal — IVF & Fertility Specialist

Dr. Deepa Chudal (MBBS, MD Obstetrics & Gynecology, Fellowship in Reproductive Medicine) is a renowned fertility expert practicing in Kathmandu. With over a decade of focused experience in assisted reproductive technology, she provides holistic, evidence-based fertility care. Dr. Chudal's approach integrates the latest advances in reproductive medicine — ovarian reserve optimization, tailored stimulation protocols, and advanced embryology techniques — while ensuring emotional support throughout the journey.


Working Clinics: Dr. Deepa consults at Nepal Police Hospital (Maharajgunj), Shankhamul Health Care (evening hours), and Maya IVF Clinic (Kupandole). These centres provide comprehensive diagnostic facilities, infertility workup, and dedicated IVF services under her supervision.


Her expertise includes IVF/ICSI, blastocyst culture, fertility preservation (egg & embryo vitrification), PGT-A (preimplantation genetic testing), and management of recurrent implantation failure. She is a strong advocate for transparent communication and patient empowerment.

Understanding IVF: Step‑by‑Step

1. Initial Consultation

Comprehensive fertility assessment, semen analysis, hormonal panel, and ovarian reserve testing (AMH/AFC).

2. Controlled Ovarian Stimulation

Customized gonadotropin protocol to develop multiple follicles, monitored by ultrasound & endocrine tests.

3. Egg Retrieval (OPU)

Ultrasound-guided follicular aspiration under mild sedation — safe and minimally invasive.

4. Fertilization & ICSI

Conventional IVF or intracytoplasmic sperm injection; time‑lapse incubator for embryo development.

5. Embryo Assessment + AI

AI-assisted embryo selection (FEMI model) to identify highest implantation potential embryos.

6. Embryo Transfer & Luteal Support

Single embryo transfer (SET) per ASRM guidelines to maximize safety and live birth.

Recent Breakthrough: AI in IVF (2025–2026)

FEMI — Foundational Model for Embryo Imaging

Published in Nature Communications (July 2025), researchers developed FEMI, a vision transformer trained on 18 million time‑lapse embryo images. This AI model predicts blastocyst quality, ploidy status, and implantation potential with unprecedented consistency. At Dr. Chudal's partner labs, AI-assisted embryo ranking is being implemented to improve live birth rates and reduce miscarriage risk.


Why it matters: Standardized embryo grading + reduction in human variability leads to higher pregnancy rates per transfer.

Reference: Rajendran et al. “A foundational model for IVF trained on 18 million images.” Nat Commun 16, 2025.
