Adolescent Gynecology (Ages 12-19)

Building a foundation for lifelong reproductive health. Dr. Deepa Chudal provides a comfortable, confidential environment for young women.

  • Menstrual disorders: Irregular periods, heavy bleeding, painful periods (dysmenorrhea), absent periods (amenorrhea)
  • Premenstrual syndrome (PMS) & PMDD — mood and physical symptom management
  • PCOS/PMOS — early diagnosis and lifestyle intervention
  • HPV Vaccination (Gardasil-9) — cervical cancer prevention
  • Contraception counseling — for medically indicated cases
  • Reproductive health education — body literacy and puberty guidance

When to Consult

  • No period by age 15 (primary amenorrhea)
  • Periods <21 days or >45 days apart
  • Bleeding >7 days or soaking pad every hour
  • Severe pain not relieved by over-the-counter meds
  • Concerns about development or discharge
Confidential care: Adolescent visits can be conducted with or without parents per patient preference.

Reproductive Years Care (Ages 20-40)

Annual Well-Woman Exam

Comprehensive preventive care: pelvic exam, clinical breast exam, Pap smear/LBC, HPV testing, and general health assessment.

Contraception Services

Oral contraceptives, IUD (copper/hormonal), implant, injectable, emergency contraception. Personalized selection.

Menstrual Health

Management of heavy bleeding, irregular cycles, dysmenorrhea, endometriosis, and fibroids. Medical & surgical options.

Fertility & Preconception

Fertility awareness, ovulation tracking, preconception counseling, and early infertility evaluation.

Infections & STI Management

Diagnosis and treatment of vaginal infections (BV, candidiasis), chlamydia, gonorrhea, and HPV management.

Expanded carrier screening for thalassemia, SMA, CF — recommended before pregnancy.

Pap smear every 3 years (21-29), Pap+HPV co-test every 5 years (30-65). Colposcopy available if abnormal.

Miscarriage & Recurrent Loss

Comprehensive evaluation and management for couples with pregnancy loss.

Comprehensive Obstetric Care

Antenatal Care (ANC)

Regular checkups, ultrasound scans (dating, anomaly, growth), blood tests, and nutritional guidance. First trimester screening, GDM testing, and fetal monitoring.

High-Risk Pregnancy Management

Expert care for gestational diabetes, preeclampsia, hypertension, multiple gestations, thyroid disorders, and previous pregnancy complications.

Normal & Assisted Delivery

Normal vaginal delivery, vacuum/forceps assisted delivery, and emergency C-section when indicated. Pain management options.

Postnatal (Postpartum) Care

Maternal recovery, breastfeeding support, postpartum depression screening, perineal care, and contraception planning after delivery.

Fetal Medicine & Monitoring

Doppler studies, non-stress test (NST), biophysical profile (BPP), and growth scans for high-risk pregnancies.

Immunizations in Pregnancy

TDaP, influenza, and COVID-19 vaccines per CDC/ACOG guidelines.

Recommended Antenatal Visit Schedule

  • First trimester (6-12 weeks): Dating scan, blood tests, thyroid profile, booking visit
  • 11-14 weeks: NT scan + combined first trimester screening
  • 18-22 weeks: Anomaly scan (detailed anatomy ultrasound)
  • 24-28 weeks: Gestational diabetes screening (OGTT)
  • 28-32 weeks: Growth scan, anti-D if Rh negative
  • 34-36 weeks: GBS swab, fetal position check, birth planning
  • 37-40 weeks: Weekly visits, CTG if indicated
  • 40-41 weeks: Post-dates monitoring, induction discussion

Perimenopause & Menopause Care (Ages 40+)

Menopause is a natural transition — not a disease. But symptoms deserve expert management. Dr. Deepa Chudal provides individualized care for women navigating this life stage.

  • Vasomotor symptoms: Hot flashes, night sweats — HRT, non-hormonal options (fezolinetant, SSRIs)
  • Sleep disturbances & mood changes: Insomnia, irritability, depression, anxiety
  • Vaginal & urinary health: Vaginal dryness, dyspareunia, recurrent UTIs, GSM (genitourinary syndrome)
  • Cardiometabolic health: Weight gain, insulin resistance, lipid profile monitoring
  • Bone health: DEXA scan, calcium/vitamin D, bisphosphonates if osteopenia/osteoporosis
  • Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT): Individualized risk-benefit assessment — transdermal estradiol + micronized progesterone

Recommended Screening After Menopause

  • Annual well-woman exam
  • Mammogram every 1-2 years (ages 50-74)
  • Bone density (DEXA) at menopause transition
  • Lipid profile, blood pressure, glucose screening
  • Pap/HPV co-test every 5 years until 65
  • Colorectal cancer screening (starting age 45-50)
Lifestyle for healthy menopause: Weight-bearing exercise, calcium-rich diet, stress reduction, pelvic floor therapy.

Common Gynecological Conditions We Treat

Uterine Fibroids

Heavy bleeding, pelvic pressure. Options: monitoring, medication, UAE, myomectomy, hysterectomy.

Endometriosis

Chronic pelvic pain, painful periods, infertility. Medical management or laparoscopic excision.

Pelvic Organ Prolapse

Cystocele, rectocele, uterine prolapse. Pelvic floor therapy, pessary, or surgical repair.

Ovarian Cysts

Functional cysts, dermoid, endometrioma. Monitoring or laparoscopic cystectomy.

Abnormal Uterine Bleeding

Comprehensive workup: ultrasound, hysteroscopy, endometrial biopsy. Medical or surgical treatment.

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS/PMOS)

Irregular cycles, hirsutism, metabolic syndrome. Lifestyle, metformin, ovulation induction.
