Cervical Cancer Screening: Pap Smear & Liquid Based Cytology (LBC)

Regular cervical screening saves lives. Cervical cancer is one of the most preventable cancers when detected early. Dr. Deepa Chudal offers both conventional Pap smear and advanced Liquid Based Cytology (LBC) — a more sensitive technique that reduces inadequate samples and allows HPV co-testing.


Who should be screened? All women aged 21-65 years. Starting at age 21, every 3 years for Pap alone, or every 5 years for HPV/Pap co-test (age 30-65).

2026 Updated Guidelines (ACS/ASCCP): Primary HPV testing every 5 years is now preferred for women ≥30. LBC with reflex HPV testing available at Dr. Chudal's clinic.

What to Expect

  • Quick, painless procedure (5 minutes)
  • Best scheduled when not menstruating
  • Avoid intercourse, douching, vaginal meds 48 hours prior
  • Results in 5-7 days
  • Abnormal results? Colposcopy available on-site
HPV Vaccination available: Gardasil-9 for ages 9-45. Prevents 90% of cervical cancers.

Menstrual Disorders: Diagnosis & Advanced Treatment

Heavy Menstrual Bleeding (HMB)

Menorrhagia causes anemia and fatigue. Options: Tranexamic acid, hormonal IUS (Mirena), endometrial ablation, or minimally invasive hysterectomy when indicated.

Irregular Periods (Oligomenorrhea)

Common in PCOS, thyroid disorders, hyperprolactinemia. Comprehensive workup: hormonal panels, pelvic ultrasound, and lifestyle interventions.

Frequent/Intermenstrual Bleeding

May indicate fibroids, polyps, or cervical pathology. Diagnostic hysteroscopy and polypectomy available.

Dysmenorrhea (Painful Periods)

Primary dysmenorrhea: NSAIDs, OCPs. Secondary dysmenorrhea: endometriosis evaluation via laparoscopy.

Absent Periods (Amenorrhea)

Primary (no period by 15) vs secondary. Workup: Progesterone challenge, FSH, prolactin, karyotype if indicated.

Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD)

Severe mood symptoms before menses. Treatment: SSRIs, OCPs, cognitive behavioral therapy.

Comprehensive Menopause & Perimenopause Care

Menopause is not a disease — but symptoms deserve expert management. Perimenopause (transition) typically begins in 40s. Dr. Chudal provides individualized care for vasomotor symptoms (hot flashes, night sweats), vaginal dryness, sleep disturbances, mood changes, and bone health.


Modern menopause management (2025-2026): Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) is safe and effective when started within 10 years of menopause. Transdermal estradiol + micronized progesterone for women with uterus. Non-hormonal options: fezolinetant (NK3 antagonist) for hot flashes, cognitive behavioral therapy, and lifestyle modifications.


Long-term health: Bone density screening (DEXA), cardiovascular risk assessment, and breast cancer surveillance integrated into menopause care.

Menopause Symptom Management

  • Hot flashes: HRT (gold standard), SSRI/SNRI, gabapentin, lifestyle (cool environment, avoid triggers)
  • Vaginal atrophy: Low-dose vaginal estrogen, moisturizers, lubricants, ospemifene
  • Sleep disturbance: Melatonin, sleep hygiene, HRT often improves sleep quality
  • Bone protection: Calcium 1200mg, Vitamin D 2000 IU, weight-bearing exercise, bisphosphonates if osteopenic
Annual Well-Woman Visit includes: Mammogram, Pap/HPV, bone density (if indicated), lipid profile, blood pressure.

Other Common Gynecological Conditions Managed

Vaginal Infections

Bacterial vaginosis, candidiasis, trichomoniasis — accurate diagnosis via microscopy/culture, targeted antibiotic/antifungal therapy.

Uterine Fibroids (Leiomyomas)

Symptomatic fibroids causing bleeding/pressure. Options: watchful waiting, medication, UAE, myomectomy, hysterectomy.

Endometriosis

Chronic pelvic pain, dysmenorrhea, infertility. Medical management (OCPs, GnRH agonists) or laparoscopic excision.

Pelvic Organ Prolapse

Cystocele, rectocele, uterine prolapse. Pelvic floor therapy, pessary, or surgical repair (sacrocolpopexy).

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS/PMOS)

Irregular cycles, hirsutism, metabolic syndrome. Lifestyle, metformin, anti-androgens, ovulation induction.

Contraception Counseling

OCPs, IUD (copper/hormonal), implant, injection, emergency contraception. Personalized selection based on health profile.

Recommended Gynecological Screening Schedule

  • 21-29 years: Pap smear every 3 years
  • 30-65 years: Pap + HPV co-test every 5 years OR Pap alone every 3 years
  • ≥65 years: May discontinue if adequate prior negatives
  • Annual well-woman exam: Clinical breast exam, pelvic exam, blood pressure, BMI assessment
  • Mammogram: Starting age 40-45 every 1-2 years
  • Bone density (DEXA): At menopause transition, earlier if risk factors
