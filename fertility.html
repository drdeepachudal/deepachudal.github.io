Ovarian Reserve & Hormonal Profiling

Anti-Müllerian Hormone (AMH)

Gold standard for ovarian reserve. AMH levels correlate with antral follicle count. Predicts response to ovarian stimulation.
Normal: 1.0-4.0 ng/mL | Low: <1.0 | High: >4.0 (PCOS)

Antral Follicle Count (AFC)

Transvaginal ultrasound assessment of follicles (2-10mm) on Day 2-4 of cycle. AFC 6-10 indicates normal reserve; <5 diminished; >20 suggests PCOS.

Day 3 FSH, LH, Estradiol

Baseline hormonal assessment. Elevated FSH (>10-12 IU/L) suggests diminished reserve. FSH/LH ratio >2 may indicate PCOS.

Progesterone (Day 21/7 DPO)

Confirms ovulation. Level >3 ng/mL indicates ovulatory cycle. <3 suggests anovulation.

Inhibin B & AMH Plus

Newer marker (2026) — inhibin B combined with AMH improves prediction of poor response to IVF. Available at partner labs.

Thyroid & Prolactin

TSH (optimal <2.5 mIU/L for conception), Free T4, prolactin. Hyperprolactinemia causes ovulatory dysfunction.

Tubal Patency & Uterine Cavity Assessment

Tubal factor accounts for 25-35% of female infertility. Accurate assessment is critical before any fertility treatment. Dr. Deepa Chudal offers:

  • Hysterosalpingography (HSG) — X-ray contrast dye to visualize fallopian tubes. Detects proximal/distal block, hydrosalpinx, intrauterine adhesions.
  • HyCoSy (Hysterosalpingo Contrast Sonography) — Ultrasound-based alternative, no radiation, evaluates tubal patency and ovarian morphology simultaneously.
  • Saline Infusion Sonography (SIS/SHG) — Saline instillation to assess uterine cavity for polyps, fibroids, adhesions.
  • Diagnostic Hysteroscopy — Direct visualization of uterine cavity, gold standard for intrauterine pathology.

Test Comparison

HyCoSyNo radiation, assesses tubes & ovaries, operator dependent

Male Fertility Assessment: Semen Analysis & Beyond

Standard Semen Analysis (WHO 2026)

Volume ≥1.5mL, Concentration ≥15M/mL, Total count ≥39M, Motility ≥40%, Progressive motility ≥32%, Normal morphology ≥4% (Kruger strict).

Sperm DNA Fragmentation Index (DFI)

High DFI (>25-30%) associated with recurrent miscarriage and failed IVF. Test indicated for unexplained infertility, recurrent loss.

Advanced Sperm Testing

Sperm aneuploidy, acrosome reaction test, oxidative stress markers. Available on clinical indication.

Semen Culture & Antibiotic Sensitivity

For leukocytospermia or suspected genital tract infection.

Hormonal Evaluation for Male

FSH, LH, testosterone, prolactin. Abnormal levels indicate hypogonadism or obstruction.

Scrotal Doppler Ultrasound

For varicocele detection — most common correctable cause of male infertility.

Important: Semen sample requires 2-5 days abstinence. Two samples analyzed 2-4 weeks apart for reliable diagnosis.

Genetic & Advanced Diagnostic Testing (2025-2026)

Carrier Screening for Couples

Expanded carrier screening for 500+ autosomal recessive disorders (thalassemia, SMA, CF). Recommended before IVF or preconception.

Karyotype & Fragile X

For primary ovarian insufficiency, recurrent miscarriage, severe male factor (azoospermia).

Endometrial Receptivity Array (ERA)

For recurrent implantation failure — identifies personalized embryo transfer window.

Endometrial Microbiome Test (EMMA/ALICE)

Identifies chronic endometritis and optimal Lactobacillus dominance for implantation.

ReceptivaDx (BCL6)

Marker for endometriosis-associated infertility without surgery. Non-invasive endometrial biopsy.

Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT-A)

For couples undergoing IVF — screens embryos for aneuploidy. Discuss candidacy with Dr. Chudal.
