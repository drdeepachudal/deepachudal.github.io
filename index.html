Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of IVF treatment in Nepal?
IVF treatment ranges from NPR 1.5 to 2.5 lakhs. IUI costs around NPR 15k–25k.
When should I see a fertility specialist?
If under 35 trying for 1+ year, or over 35 trying for 6+ months, or have PCOS/endometriosis.
What is the success rate of IUI and IVF?
IUI success rates: 10-20% per cycle. IVF success rates: 40-55% depending on age.
Does Dr. Deepa Chudal offer laparoscopic surgery?
Yes, she specializes in minimally invasive gynecological surgeries.
