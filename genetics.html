Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT): Comprehensive Overview

PGT involves testing embryos created through IVF for genetic abnormalities before transfer. This advanced technology helps select healthy embryos, reduces miscarriage risk, and prevents transmission of inherited disorders. Dr. Deepa Chudal offers all three PGT modalities:

  • PGT-A (Aneuploidy Screening) — Chromosome number abnormalities (Down syndrome, Turner syndrome, etc.)
  • PGT-M (Monogenic/Single Gene) — Specific inherited disorders (thalassemia, cystic fibrosis, SMA, Huntington's)
  • PGT-SR (Structural Rearrangements) — Chromosomal translocations, inversions, deletions
Process: IVF → blastocyst culture (Day 5-6) → trophectoderm biopsy (5-10 cells) → genetic analysis (2-3 weeks) → freeze-all → transfer euploid/unaffected embryo in subsequent cycle.

Who Benefits from PGT?

  • Advanced maternal age (≥35 years)
  • Recurrent pregnancy loss (≥2 miscarriages)
  • Repeated IVF implantation failure
  • Known parental chromosomal translocation
  • Carriers of single-gene disorders
  • Severe male factor infertility
  • Previous child with aneuploidy
Counseling required: Pre-PGT genetic counseling with Dr. Chudal and geneticist included.

Preconception & Prenatal Genetic Screening

Expanded Carrier Screening (ECS) 600+ Diseases

Screens for autosomal recessive and X-linked disorders. Recommended for all couples planning pregnancy, regardless of ethnicity. Identifies carrier status for thalassemia, cystic fibrosis, spinal muscular atrophy, fragile X, and more.

Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing (NIPT/NIPS)

Cell-free fetal DNA from maternal blood after 10 weeks. Screens for trisomy 21,18,13, sex chromosome aneuploidies, and select microdeletions. >99% sensitivity for Down syndrome.

Karyotype & Chromosomal Microarray (CMA)

For couples with recurrent miscarriage, infertility, or known structural anomalies. CMA detects submicroscopic deletions/duplications not visible on standard karyotype.

Cell-Free DNA for Monogenic Disorders

Expanded NIPT (2025-2026) now screens for dominant monogenic conditions like achondroplasia, Noonan syndrome, and Rett syndrome.

PGT for Aneuploidy (PGT-A) with NGS

Next-generation sequencing (NGS) provides highest resolution for detecting mosaicism and segmental aneuploidies. Available at partner labs.

Fragile X & SMA Universal Screening

ACOG recommends offering fragile X carrier screening and spinal muscular atrophy carrier screening to all women considering pregnancy.

Is Genetic Testing Right for You?

Dr. Deepa Chudal recommends comprehensive genetic consultation for:

  • Couples with family history of genetic disorders

Ethical Framework for PGT & Genetic Testing

Dr. Deepa Chudal adheres to international ethical guidelines:

  • ASRM / ESHRE / PGDIS recommendations (2025-2026)
  • Pre-test and post-test genetic counseling mandatory
  • Informed consent for all genetic procedures
  • Transparent discussion of limitations (mosaicism, inconclusive results)
  • No sex selection for non-medical reasons (per Nepal law)

PGT Limitations to Understand:

  • Mosaicism: 2-5% of embryos — repeat testing or transfer with caution
  • No test is 100% — confirmatory prenatal diagnosis recommended
  • PGT does not detect all genetic conditions
  • Embryo biopsy requires vitrification → freeze-all cycle
