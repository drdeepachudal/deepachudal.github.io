Understanding the Range

Costs vary based on:

  • Clinic location and laboratory facilities
  • Required medication dosage (age/ovarian reserve dependent)
  • Need for ICSI vs conventional IVF
  • Genetic testing (PGT) requirements
  • Number of IVF cycles (multi-cycle packages available)

Hidden Costs & Additional Expenses to Know

  • Medications: Often the most variable cost (₹30k-70k). Dosage depends on age, AMH, and protocol.
  • Embryo Storage: Annual storage fee after first year (₹8k-15k/year).
  • Additional Monitoring: Extra ultrasounds/blood tests if cycle prolonged.
  • Anesthesia Fees: For egg retrieval (usually included, confirm with clinic).
  • ICSI for all eggs: Some clinics charge per egg — confirm pricing model.
  • Donor Screening: Additional tests for anonymous donors.

What's Usually Included?

  • Cycle monitoring (3-5 ultrasounds + blood tests)
  • Egg retrieval procedure + OT charges
  • Embryology lab fees (culture, media, incubators)
  • Embryo transfer procedure
  • Basic follow-up after transfer (beta hCG test)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IVF covered by insurance in Nepal?

Most insurance plans do not cover IVF procedures, but some corporate plans now include infertility diagnostics. Dr. Chudal's clinic provides documentation for reimbursement claims.

Are medications included in the IVF package?

Most clinics exclude medication costs as they vary by patient. Budget ₹30,000-70,000 separately for injectable gonadotropins and other meds.

Can I get a discount for multiple cycles?

Yes, multi-cycle packages offer significant savings. Dr. Chudal's clinic provides transparent multi-cycle pricing — ask during consultation.

What is the refund policy if cycle is cancelled?

Policies vary. Most clinics refund a portion if cycle cancelled before egg retrieval. Always review cancellation policy in writing before starting.
