Intrauterine Insemination (IUI) — First-Line Assisted Reproduction

IUI involves placing washed, concentrated sperm directly into the uterine cavity around the time of ovulation. It's a simple, minimally invasive, and affordable first-line treatment for:

  • Unexplained infertility
  • Mild male factor (low count/motility)
  • Cervical factor infertility
  • Same-sex couples using donor sperm
  • Anovulation (with ovulation induction)

Procedure: Ovulation induction (oral or injectable meds) → ultrasound monitoring → IUI at 36-40 hours post-trigger → luteal phase support.

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) & Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI)

Conventional IVF

Eggs and sperm are co-incubated in a dish allowing natural fertilization. Ideal for unexplained infertility, tubal factor, mild male factor.

ICSI Gold Standard for Male Factor

Single sperm injected directly into each mature egg. Essential for severe male factor (low count, poor motility, abnormal morphology), prior IVF fertilization failure, or using frozen sperm.

Blastocyst Culture & Transfer

Embryos cultured to Day 5-6 (blastocyst stage). Higher implantation rates per transfer, allowing single embryo transfer (SET) to reduce multiples.

Vitrification (Egg/Embryo Freezing)

Ultra-rapid freezing technology with >95% survival rate. For fertility preservation (egg freezing) or surplus embryo storage.

Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET)

Natural or programmed cycles for thawed embryo transfer. FET cycles have equivalent or higher success rates than fresh transfers in some populations.

Assisted Hatching (AH)

Laser thinning of zona pellucida to improve implantation for poor prognosis patients (advanced age, elevated FSH, prior failed cycles).

Advanced & Specialized ART Procedures

Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT-A/PGT-M)

PGT-A screens embryos for chromosome aneuploidy — increases live birth rates, reduces miscarriage. PGT-M for single gene disorders (thalassemia, cystic fibrosis, SMA).

AI-Powered Embryo Selection (FEMI)

Foundational model trained on 18M+ embryo images predicts implantation potential with unprecedented accuracy — improves pregnancy rates per transfer.

Donor Egg & Donor Sperm Program

Anonymous or known donor options with rigorous screening. For diminished ovarian reserve, genetic disorders, or same-sex couples.

Fertility Preservation (Egg/Embryo Freezing)

For medical indications (cancer, endometriosis) or social/elective freezing. Oocyte cryopreservation with vitrification — success comparable to fresh eggs.

Gestational Surrogacy

Embryo transfer to a gestational carrier. Available for women with uterine factor, recurrent implantation failure, or medical contraindication to pregnancy.

IVF with PGT-A & Endometrial Receptivity Array

Personalized embryo transfer timing (ERA) + euploid embryo selection — highest success for recurrent implantation failure.

The IVF Journey: Step-by-Step

1. Initial Consultation & Workup

Fertility assessment, AMH, AFC, semen analysis, HSG. Personalized treatment plan.

2. Ovarian Stimulation

8-12 days of injectable gonadotropins. Ultrasound/blood monitoring to track follicle growth.

3. Trigger Shot

hCG or GnRH agonist to mature eggs — given 36 hours before egg retrieval.

4. Egg Retrieval (OPU)

15-20 min ultrasound-guided procedure under sedation. Average 8-15 eggs retrieved.

5. Fertilization (IVF/ICSI)

Eggs combined with sperm. ICSI for male factor. Fertilization check next day.

6. Embryo Culture

Embryos cultured to Day 3 (cleavage) or Day 5-6 (blastocyst) in time-lapse incubator.

7. PGT-A (optional)

Biopsy of trophectoderm cells. Genetic analysis takes 2-3 weeks. Freeze-all cycle.

8. Embryo Transfer

Fresh or frozen transfer. 1-2 embryos placed into uterus. 10-14 days later: pregnancy test.

Cutting-Edge ART Innovations (2025–2026)

AI in IVF: FEMI Model

Nature Communications 2025: Foundational embryo imaging model predicts blastocyst quality and ploidy with 87% accuracy. Available at Dr. Chudal's partner lab.

Non-Invasive PGT (niPGT)

Analysis of cell-free DNA in spent blastocyst culture media — avoids embryo biopsy. Emerging technology with promising accuracy (2026 data).

Time-Lapse Imaging + AI Morphokinetics

Continuous embryo monitoring with predictive algorithms identifies highest potential embryos without disturbing culture.

Artificial Ovarian Cortex Culture

Experimental technique for fertility restoration in cancer survivors — under evaluation at select centers.

In Vitro Maturation (IVM)

Maturation of immature oocytes retrieved without full stimulation. Reduced medication, lower OHSS risk. Available for PCOS patients.

PGT-P (Polygenic Risk Scoring)

Screening for polygenic adult-onset conditions. Ethical counseling provided before considering.

Which ART Treatment is Right for You?

Dr. Deepa Chudal personalizes treatment based on:

  • Age and ovarian reserve (AMH/AFC)
  • Duration and cause of infertility
  • Prior treatment history
  • Genetic considerations
  • Financial and emotional factors

Typical algorithm:

  • IUI: Unexplained, mild male factor, donor sperm
  • Conventional IVF: Tubal factor, unexplained, mild endometriosis
  • ICSI: Severe male factor, prior fertilization failure
  • ICSI + PGT-A: Advanced maternal age, recurrent miscarriage
  • Donor eggs: Diminished ovarian reserve, genetic disorders
