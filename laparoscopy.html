Why Laparoscopy? Minimally Invasive = Faster Recovery

Laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery uses small incisions (0.5-1cm), a high-definition camera, and specialized instruments. Compared to traditional open surgery, patients experience dramatic benefits:

Hospital Stay1-2 days4-7 days
Return to Work1-2 weeks4-6 weeks
Scar Size0.5-1 cm8-15 cm
Blood LossMinimalSignificant
Pain Post-opLowModerate-Severe

Advanced Laparoscopic Technology & 2025-2026 Innovations

4K & 3D Laparoscopic System

Ultra-high definition visualization with 3D depth perception improves surgical precision, especially for endometriosis and myomectomy.

Robotic-Assisted Laparoscopy

Enhanced dexterity and tremor filtration for complex procedures (available at select partner centers).

Laparo-Endoscopic Single Site (LESS)

Single incision through the belly button — virtually scarless surgery for ovarian cysts and appendectomy.

Fluorescence Imaging (ICG)

Indocyanine green dye to visualize blood flow in fallopian tubes, ureters, and evaluate perfusion in bowel anastomosis.

Enhanced Recovery After Surgery (ERAS) 2026

Evidence-based perioperative protocols — minimal fasting, multimodal analgesia, early feeding → discharge within 24 hours for select cases.

AI-Assisted Surgical Guidance

Real-time anatomical identification and safety zone alerts — reduces complication risk by 40% (JAMA Surg 2025).

What to Expect: Your Laparoscopic Journey

Before surgery: Pre-anesthetic checkup, basic labs, ultrasound. Most procedures done under general anesthesia.

During surgery: 2-4 small incisions. Carbon dioxide gas inflates abdomen for visibility. Procedure duration: 30-120 mins depending on complexity.

After surgery: Discharge within 24-48 hours. Pain managed with oral meds. No heavy lifting for 2 weeks. Follow-up at 1 week for stitch removal (absorbable sutures usually).

Emergency contact available 24/7 post-surgery.
